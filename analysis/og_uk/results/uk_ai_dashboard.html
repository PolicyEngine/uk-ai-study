We overshoot the level and undershoot the growth because γ steps once and is fully in force from 2026, whereas their automation builds gradually to 2030. Fixing that needs time-varying gamma, which OG-Core does not support.
Caveats. (1) The two paths do not share an origin — the γ step puts a +12.2% wedge in at 2026. Automation and a common origin cannot coexist until gamma[t,m] is patched. (2) Consumption is already +12.9% in 2026: households have perfect foresight and consume against a permanently richer future from period one. (3) Investment is unreliable — it is the residual in the resource constraint and absorbs the numerical error (RC 6.7e-03 / 7.9e-03), hence the 2030 reversal. (4) No unemployment, no search frictions, no occupational split: this run cannot speak to displacement.
